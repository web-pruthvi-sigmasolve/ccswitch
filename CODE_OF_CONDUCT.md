# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1, in short form.

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Good behaviour:

- Be kind and patient. Give and accept feedback gracefully.
- Respect different viewpoints and experience levels.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the community.

Unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome advances.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other
contributions that do not follow this code, and may ban contributors for
behaviour they judge inappropriate.

## Reporting

Report problems privately through the **Security** tab of
`github.com/ccswitch/ccswitch` (private reporting) or by contacting a
maintainer directly. All reports are reviewed and handled in confidence.
